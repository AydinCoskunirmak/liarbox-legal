Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Liarbox (the "App") is a logic puzzle game developed by Aydın Coşkunırmak ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App on iOS or Android.

Summary

  • We do not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The App may show optional ads provided by Google AdMob, which may process device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, saved (paused) puzzles, statistics, daily puzzle results and settings (language, vibration) are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us or to any server we operate. Deleting the App removes it permanently.

Advertising

The App may display optional rewarded ads, for example to get a hint or an extra heart. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

On iOS, personalized ads are only shown if you allow tracking when the App asks. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • device information such as model, operating system and language
  • approximate location derived from your IP address
  • interactions with ads

Sharing and contacting us

When you share a puzzle result or the App, the text is sent through the app you choose (for example, Messages or WhatsApp); we do not see that content. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply to you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools that identify you.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.

Children

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data on our servers, you can delete all App data by uninstalling the App. For data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy controls. For any request, email csknrmk.aydn@icloud.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this address and in the App's Settings. The "Last updated" date shows when it last changed.
