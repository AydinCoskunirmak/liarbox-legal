How to play

  • Each character says one sentence. Honest characters always tell the truth; liars always lie.
  • The rule card shows exactly how many liars there are.
  • The treasure is in exactly one character's chest. Find it.
  • Mark characters as honest or liar for free. Opening an empty chest costs a heart; after 3 mistakes the game is over.
  • Every puzzle has exactly one logical solution. No guessing is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Will my progress sync between devices? No. Progress is stored on your device only.

I left a game halfway. Is it lost? No. Tap the same difficulty on the home screen and choose Continue.

How do I change the language? Settings → Language.

How do I delete my data? Delete the App. Nothing is stored on our servers.
